Best Places to Buy Clothes in Ghana

Last verified Aug 2026 · 7 min read

Clothes shopping in Ghana isn't just one market — it splits into a few genuinely different worlds depending on budget, style, and whether you want something mass-produced or made specifically for you. Here's how they compare.

Jumia

Best for fast fashion + brand variety

Jumia's clothing section is enormous — international fast-fashion brands alongside Ankara and African-print pieces, sortable by size, price and brand. Same buyer protection and cash-on-delivery as their electronics side, which matters more with clothing since sizing can be unpredictable.

Pros
  • Huge brand selection
  • Buyer protection on disputes
  • Cash on delivery
Cons
  • Sizing varies a lot by brand
  • Mostly mass-produced, not unique

Kantamanto & secondhand markets

Best value, most sustainable

Ghana's secondhand clothing markets — Kantamanto in Accra is the most famous — are genuinely one of the best-value clothing sources in the country, known locally as "obroni wawu." You're sorting through bales yourself, so it takes more time and patience, but the prices and the chance of finding something genuinely unique are unmatched by anything online.

Pros
  • Extremely low prices
  • Unique, one-off finds
  • Sustainable/secondhand
Cons
  • Takes time to sort through
  • No sizing guarantee — try before buying
  • Not available online, in-person only

Local tailors & designers

Best for custom fit, kente & traditional wear

For anything traditional — kente, smocks, custom-fitted formal wear — a local tailor beats any online store. This is where Ghana's fashion scene genuinely shines: made-to-measure at prices that would be unaffordable in most countries, and the only real way to get authentic handwoven kente rather than a printed imitation.

Pros
  • Perfect custom fit
  • Authentic traditional wear
  • Supports local craftspeople directly
Cons
  • Takes longer (fittings, production time)
  • Quality varies by tailor — ask around for recommendations

Ubuy

Best for international/premium brands

If you specifically want a name-brand piece — Nike, Levi's, and similar international labels — Ubuy is a cross-border option that ships these into Ghana, which Jumia's catalogue doesn't cover as deeply on the premium end. Expect higher prices and longer delivery than local options, since you're paying for import.

Pros
  • Access to major international brands
  • Wide premium selection
Cons
  • Higher prices (import costs)
  • Longer delivery times

Bottom line

For everyday basics and fast fashion, Jumia is the easy default. For genuinely great value and one-of-a-kind pieces, it's worth making time for Kantamanto in person. For anything traditional or that needs to fit perfectly, a local tailor beats every online option. And if it has to be a specific international brand, Ubuy fills that gap.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kantamanto worth the trip?

For value and unique pieces, yes — it's one of the best secondhand markets around. Go with cash, some time to dig, and a rough price in mind so you can negotiate confidently.

How do I find a good tailor?

Word of mouth beats anything else — ask people whose clothes you admire who made them. Bring a clear reference (a photo or an existing garment), agree on price and timeline upfront, and expect at least one fitting before the final piece.

Is buying from Ubuy or other international sites reliable?

Generally yes for getting a specific brand you can't find locally, but factor in longer shipping times and import charges on top of the sticker price — it's rarely the cheapest option, just the most direct one for brand-specific items.
