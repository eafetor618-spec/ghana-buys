Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026
Note: This policy is a general-purpose draft, not legal advice. If you need it to meet specific legal requirements, have it reviewed by a lawyer.

What this site is

Ghana Buys is an independent buying-guides website. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, and how it's used.

Information we collect

If you sign up for our email updates, we collect and store the email address you provide, using Supabase as our database provider. We don't collect your name, phone number, or any other personal detail as part of that signup. Browsing the site itself doesn't require you to submit any personal information.

Email updates

Signing up for email updates is optional and opt-in only — we never add you without your action. We only use your email to send occasional new-guide or price-update notices, never to sell or share with third parties. To have your email removed, contact us using the details on the site and we'll delete it from our database.

Cookies and tracking

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may set cookies or collect technical data (like your browser type) as part of delivering that content. If this site displays advertising in the future (such as Google AdSense), those ad providers may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

Affiliate links

This site contains affiliate links, including to Jumia. If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The retailer (e.g. Jumia) handles your order, payment, and any data you provide during checkout — we don't see or store that information. Review the retailer's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

Third-party links

This site links to external websites (retailers, stores, and other resources). We aren't responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites once you leave Ghana Buys.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site grows — for example, if we add analytics, ads, or account features. Check back periodically for changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details listed on the site.
