How to actually choose

Don't just chase specs. Match the phone to what you'll actually do with it:

  • Just calls, WhatsApp, MoMo, browsing? The itel A70 or Infinix Smart 10 are more than enough — save your money.
  • Care about photos, social media? The Tecno Spark 30 is the clear pick in this range.
  • Planning to keep it 2+ years? The Samsung A05s tends to age better thanks to longer update support.
  • Want to avoid a counterfeit or "grey market" unit? Buy from a verified seller with buyer protection (like Jumia) rather than an unverified third-party listing — it's the single biggest way people get burned on phone purchases in Ghana.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to buy a phone online in Ghana?

Yes, as long as you buy from a store with real buyer protection — like Jumia, where you can pay on delivery in many areas and return a faulty unit. Be far more cautious with random social media sellers or unverified third-party listings.

What's the real difference between itel, Infinix and Tecno?

All three are owned by the same parent company (Transsion) and share a lot of the same hardware. In practice, itel targets the tightest budgets, Infinix usually offers the best value for the money, and Tecno leans into camera and display quality at a slightly higher price.

Should I buy a used or refurbished phone instead?

It can save real money, but check the IMEI is clean and matches the box, insist on testing the phone (camera, charging port, battery health) before paying, and only buy from a seller who'll let you return it if something's wrong.
